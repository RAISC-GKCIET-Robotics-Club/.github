## ⚡ WHO WE ARE

**RAISC** is a multidisciplinary student technology community focused on
**building intelligent, autonomous and real-world engineering systems.**

We bring together students who want to:

`EXPLORE` → `LEARN` → `BUILD` → `COMPETE` → `RESEARCH` → `INNOVATE`
